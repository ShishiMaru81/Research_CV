# Week 9 — Paper writing

## Goal

Assemble a complete manuscript draft from the frozen Week 8 results. No new
training or selective reruns.

## Inputs (immutable)

- `frozen_results/` summary CSVs and audit report
- `paper/figures/fig01`–`fig09`
- `paper/tables/*.csv` and `*.tex`
- `paper/draft_datasets.md`, `paper/draft_mitigation.md`
- `notes/week6_retro.md`, `notes/week7_retro.md`, `notes/week8_retro.md`

## Deliverable

- `paper/manuscript.md` — full draft: Abstract through Conclusion
- Venue-agnostic Markdown suitable for later conversion to LaTeX/Word

## Definition of done

- All three contribution layers (benchmark, diagnosis, mitigation) are written
- Every major claim cites a frozen table or figure
- Limitations are stated honestly (one seed, one split, Grad-CAM overlay gap)
- `notes/week9_retro.md` records draft status
