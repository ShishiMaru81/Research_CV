# Progress Report — Cross-Dataset Generalization of Bangladeshi Rice Leaf Disease Models

Prepared: 2026-08-01. Every claim below is traceable to a specific file, commit, or number already checked into this repository. File paths are relative to `Research_CV/` unless stated otherwise. Full 40-character commit hashes are given so each can be independently checked out and inspected (`git show <hash>`).

## 0. What this project is

A solo research project studying whether rice leaf disease classifiers trained on one Bangladeshi image dataset generalize to two other Bangladeshi datasets collected under different conditions. It has three layers, stated in [README.md](../README.md):
1. **Benchmark** — a cross-dataset transfer matrix and a generalization-gap metric.
2. **Diagnosis** — Grad-CAM attention analysis and a background-confound test (does the model key on the leaf, or on the photo background?).
3. **Mitigation** — strong data augmentation and leave-one-dataset-out (LODO) training, tested against the benchmark.

Repository ground rule, from [.cursorrules](../.cursorrules): *"NEVER fabricate, hardcode, or mock results. All numbers must be computed by the code."* All numbers cited in this report come from CSV/JSON files generated by that code, not from memory or estimation.

## 2. Week-by-week record

### Week 1 — Setup and data inventory (commit `713b1f8`)
- Inventoried three raw sources (see §6 for on-disk verification):
  - **RiceLeafBD**: 1,560 images — Bacterial Leaf Blight 422, Brown Spot 356, Healthy Leaf 252, Tungro Virus 530. Proof: [notes/dataset_inventory.md](dataset_inventory.md).
  - **Dhan-Shomadhan**: 1,106 images across 8 raw classes (including near-duplicate spelling variants, e.g. "Brown Spot" vs "Browon Spot", "Rice Tungro" vs "Rice Turgro").
  - **BRRI Rice Disease & Pest**: 2,769 on disk, 2,753 used (16-image "Rice" folder excluded — general images, not a disease class, per Mendeley/Data-in-Brief documentation).
- Defined canonical label set (10 classes) and full raw→canonical mapping table, with justification for every merge. Proof: [notes/label_mapping.md](label_mapping.md).
- Defined non-negotiable data rules (originals only, freeze splits after Week 2, halt on unmapped class). Proof: [notes/data_rules.md](data_rules.md).

### Week 2 — Harmonization (commit `cac2a58`)
- Built `src/build_manifest.py` (unified manifest) and `src/dedup.py` (cross-dataset perceptual-hash dedup).
- Dedup threshold tuning was empirical, not assumed: threshold 5 produced 5 candidate pairs that were manually inspected and rejected as false positives (similar white-background photography, different classes); threshold 2 produced 0 candidate pairs and was adopted. Proof: [notes/dedup_report.md](dedup_report.md).
- Result: canonical manifest = **5,419** unique images, 0 cross-dataset duplicates at threshold 2.
- Hit and fixed a real bug: an empty duplicate-collision list crashed `sort_values`; documented in the retro rather than silently patched. Proof: [notes/week2_retro.md](week2_retro.md).
- Smoke-tested the data loader; verified deterministic class→index mapping (`brown_spot=0`, `tungro=1`, …).

### Week 3 — Training/eval pipeline (commits `a9d9aab`, `cc1ec54`)
- Built `src/train.py` (timm backbone, frozen-head warm-up then full fine-tune, class-weighted cross-entropy, AMP, early stopping on val macro-F1) and `src/eval.py` (metrics JSON, confusion matrix, `experiment_log.csv`).
- Fixed a Windows/Kaggle path-resolution bug for image loading (commit `cc1ec54`, separate from the feature commit — a real bug found and fixed, not folded silently into the feature).
- Gate before proceeding: RiceLeafBD baseline macro-F1 in the 0.85–0.95 band. Actual result (frozen): **MobileNetV2 / RiceLeafBD / seed 42 → accuracy 0.8936, macro-F1 0.9070, n=235** — gate passed. Proof: [frozen_results/indataset_results.csv](../frozen_results/indataset_results.csv), row 1.

### Week 5 — Cross-dataset transfer matrix (commit `f0007e7`, results in `8459dbd`)
- Protocol fixed in advance ([notes/week5_plan.md](week5_plan.md)): 6 ordered source→target pairs (only pairs with ≥1 shared class), 3 models, seed 42, evaluated only on the shared-class subset.
- Frozen output: [frozen_results/crossdataset_matrix.csv](../frozen_results/crossdataset_matrix.csv), 18 data rows. Sample: RiceLeafBD→BRRI, MobileNetV2, classes {healthy, tungro}: accuracy 0.3465, macro-F1 0.3331, n=127 — the largest cross-dataset drop in the matrix.
- Gap computed and frozen separately: [frozen_results/generalization_gap.csv](../frozen_results/generalization_gap.csv), 18 rows, formula `gap = in_dataset_macro_f1 − cross_macro_f1`. Largest observed gap: RiceLeafBD→BRRI, MobileNetV2, **gap = 0.6290** (in-dataset 0.9620 → cross 0.3331).
- Manuscript aggregate: mean in-subset macro-F1 0.824, mean cross-dataset macro-F1 **0.436**, mean gap **0.387**.

### Week 6 — Diagnosis: Grad-CAM and background confound (commit `6b846fb`, results in `b6a9285`)
- Pre-registered hypothesis and pass/fail criterion, stated *before* running the test ([notes/week6_plan.md](week6_plan.md)): white-background test accuracy should exceed both field-background-same-dataset and cross-dataset field accuracy, if background is acting as a shortcut.
- Representative model: ResNet50, trained on Dhan-Shomadhan, classes {brown_spot, tungro}, seed 42.
- Frozen result, [frozen_results/background_confound.csv](../frozen_results/background_confound.csv), 3 rows:

  | condition | n | accuracy | macro_f1 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Dhan white-bg | 29 | 0.862 | 0.854 |
  | Dhan field-bg | 22 | 0.727 | 0.705 |
  | RiceLeafBD field (cross-dataset) | 133 | 0.586 | 0.573 |

  Criterion **met**. Per-class detail: brown-spot F1 degrades monotonically 0.818 → 0.625 → 0.495 across the three conditions — the same direction as the aggregate, so it isn't an artifact of class averaging.
- Grad-CAM: 12 sample records frozen in [frozen_results/gradcam_records.csv](../frozen_results/gradcam_records.csv), including a "border-attention enrichment" metric (mean heatmap value in the outer band ÷ mean over the whole image; 1.0 = uniform). Honest negative finding recorded in [notes/week6_retro.md](week6_retro.md): this metric did **not** cleanly separate correct from incorrect predictions — attention often spread onto neighboring leaves/background, but the enrichment number alone wasn't diagnostic. This limitation is carried into the manuscript rather than dropped.

### Week 7 — Mitigation: augmentation and LODO (commit `fa394b4`, results in `13b8552`)
- Strong augmentation pipeline implemented as a named, inspectable function: `src/data_loader.py::strong_train_transform` (RandomResizedCrop, HFlip, Affine, brightness/contrast, hue/saturation, Gaussian blur, coarse dropout), tagged `aug-strong` in outputs so baseline and augmented runs are never silently mixed.
- Augmented transfer matrix: [frozen_results/crossdataset_matrix_aug.csv](../frozen_results/crossdataset_matrix_aug.csv) (18 rows) and gap: [frozen_results/generalization_gap_aug.csv](../frozen_results/generalization_gap_aug.csv) (18 rows).
- Paired comparison (baseline vs. augmented, same 18 cells): [frozen_results/mitigation_pairwise_aug.csv](../frozen_results/mitigation_pairwise_aug.csv). Result, stated exactly as computed in [notes/week7_retro.md](week7_retro.md):
  - Cross-dataset macro-F1 improved in **14 of 18** matched pairs; gap reduced in **13 of 18**.
  - Mean cross macro-F1: 0.436 → 0.503 (**+0.067**). Mean gap: 0.387 → 0.333 (**−0.055**).
  - By target dataset: BRRI +0.092, RiceLeafBD +0.064, Dhan-Shomadhan +0.045.
  - Best single model: ResNet50 (mean cross macro-F1 0.609, mean gap 0.244). Largest single gain: BRRI→RiceLeafBD, ResNet50, 0.300→0.716 (+0.416). Largest single **regression**: BRRI→RiceLeafBD, MobileNetV2, 0.573→0.273 — reported, not hidden, because it contradicts the average trend.
- LODO (leave-one-dataset-out training): [frozen_results/lodo_results.csv](../frozen_results/lodo_results.csv), 9 rows. Compared against single-source baseline in [frozen_results/mitigation_comparison.csv](../frozen_results/mitigation_comparison.csv): LODO improved the baseline in only **3 of 9** cells (EfficientNet-B0/Dhan-Shomadhan +0.171, EfficientNet-B0/RiceLeafBD +0.067, MobileNetV2/RiceLeafBD +0.025). Mean LODO macro-F1 by held-out target: RiceLeafBD 0.500, Dhan-Shomadhan 0.402, BRRI 0.203 (worst — best BRRI LODO run only 0.267).
- Conclusion drawn directly from these numbers: augmentation is a more reliable mitigation than LODO for this problem.
